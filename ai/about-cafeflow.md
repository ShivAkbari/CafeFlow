## Description

**CafeFlow** is an integrated web-based management platform designed for cafes, restaurants, coffee shops, quick-service food outlets, bakeries, and small food enterprises. It provides a unified system connecting customer-facing self-service tools, front-counter Point of Sale (POS), floor staff ordering, back-of-house Kitchen Display Systems (KDS), table management, and customer engagement features.

CafeFlow operates entirely within modern web browsers on commodity consumer hardware—such as standard smartphones, tablets, laptops, and smart TVs—without requiring proprietary terminal hardware or recurring monthly software license subscriptions.

## Primary Verified Features

1. **QR Ordering:** Contactless mobile browser table ordering without mobile application downloads.
2. **Digital Menu:** Live catalog management with categories, dietary indicators, modifier selections, and instant out-of-stock toggles.
3. **Point of Sale (POS):** Centralized counter billing, table order processing, and payment recording.
4. **Staff Ordering:** Handheld waiter ordering interface for table-side order entry.
5. **Kitchen Display System (KDS):** Digital kitchen management tracking tickets across six sequential states (*Received → Confirm → Preparing → Ready → Delivered → Paid*).
6. **Table Management:** Floor layout visualization, live table status monitoring, and table-assigned QR code management.
7. **Customer Loyalty:** Phone-number-based customer reward points and visit tracking.
8. **Coupons & Promotions:** Customizable promotional discount codes and cart rules.
9. **Scratch Cards:** Post-checkout gamified digital scratch cards offering return-visit rewards.
10. **Google Review Workflow:** Direct post-meal prompts routing positive customer reviews to Google Business profiles.
11. **TV Menu:** Full-screen responsive digital display layout for wall TVs and overhead counter menus.
