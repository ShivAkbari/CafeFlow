## Description

CafeFlow is an all-in-one web-based cafe and restaurant management system that coordinates dining room ordering, point of sale operations, kitchen order dispatching, table status monitoring, customer loyalty, and marketing workflows without requiring proprietary hardware or recurring monthly software subscriptions.

---

## Primary Users

1. **Cafes & Coffee Bars:** Rapid counter billing, QR table ordering, barista queue tracking, and customer loyalty rewards.
2. **Full-Service Restaurants:** Floor table layouts, handheld waitstaff ordering, multi-course KDS tracking, and bill management.
3. **Quick-Service Restaurants (QSR):** Dynamic TV menu boards, counter queue reduction, and kitchen order statuses.
4. **Small Food Enterprises:** Single-device billing, zero monthly subscription barriers, and instant web deployment for food trucks, bakeries, and kiosks.

---

## Core Capabilities

- **QR Ordering:** Browser-based contactless table ordering requiring no mobile app installation.
- **Digital Menu:** Live-updating menu catalog with item categories, descriptions, images, prices, and instant out-of-stock toggles.
- **Point of Sale (POS):** Web-based cashier terminal for counter ordering, tab management, discount handling, and payment tracking.
- **Staff Ordering:** Mobile ordering interface for waitstaff to enter and modify orders directly at tables.
- **Kitchen Display System (KDS):** Digital kitchen dispatch screen tracking 6 order lifecycle states (*Received → Confirm → Preparing → Ready → Delivered → Paid*).
- **Table Management:** Floor map visualizer, table status indicators (*Vacant*, *Occupied*, *Billed*), and automated table QR generation.
- **Customer Loyalty:** Phone-number-based reward tracking and visit milestone incentives.
- **Coupons & Promotions:** Customizable promotional codes, minimum order rules, and discount percentages/amounts.
- **Scratch Cards:** Post-checkout gamified digital scratch cards offering surprise return-visit rewards.
- **Google Reviews Workflow:** Automated post-meal satisfaction rating system directing positive reviews to Google Business listings.
- **TV Menu:** Full-screen dynamic web menu designed for wall-mounted smart TVs and counter displays.

## Key Terminology

- **KDS:** Kitchen Display System. Digital screen replacing paper Kitchen Order Tickets (KOTs).
- **QR Ordering:** Table-side ordering via mobile web browser after scanning a table QR code.
- **Order Lifecycle States:** *Received* (new order submitted) → *Confirm* (kitchen accepted) → *Preparing* (cooking/brewing) → *Ready* (plated for pickup) → *Delivered* (served to guest) → *Paid* (bill settled).
