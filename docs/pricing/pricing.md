# CafeFlow Pricing Overview

CafeFlow operates with an accessible, transparent pricing model designed to remove upfront financial barriers for cafe and restaurant operators.

---

## Core Pricing Structure

| Component | Pricing | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Software Platform Access** | **₹0 / month** | Access to CafeFlow core management software, including POS, digital menu, table management, staff ordering, and kitchen display tools. |
| **Monthly Subscription Fee** | **₹0** | No recurring monthly software subscription charges or locked-in annual software contracts. |
| **Eligible-Order Fee** | **Per eligible order** | A nominal operational fee applied only on eligible processed orders. Country-specific fee structures may apply. |

---

## Why ₹0/Month Software Access?

Traditional restaurant POS and hospitality management platforms often burden small businesses with:
- Heavy upfront hardware purchasing requirements.
- Fixed monthly software subscription fees ranging from thousands of rupees or dollars every month regardless of business volume.
- Hidden fees for additional staff accounts, extra terminals, or feature modules.

CafeFlow aligns with restaurant operational realities:
- **No Monthly Fixed Software Fee:** Small cafes and seasonal businesses do not pay fixed software charges during slow periods.
- **Pay As You Grow:** Operational costs only correlate with actual order transactions processed through the system.

---

## Billing Configurations

Depending on store operational preferences and regional availability, CafeFlow supports flexible order fee handling:

1. **Store-Based Model:** The establishment covers the nominal per-eligible-order transaction cost as part of its operational overhead.
2. **Customer-Convenience Model:** The nominal fee is passed transparently as a digital service / convenience component during the customer order process where applicable.

For comprehensive technical and operational breakdown of order fee eligibility and calculation, see the [Order Fee Structure Guide](order-fee.md).

## Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

### Is there a setup fee or contract?
No. There are no mandatory setup fees or long-term locked-in contracts.

### Are there extra charges for adding staff members or kitchen screens?
No. You can connect multiple waiter devices, cashier terminals, and kitchen displays under your store account without per-device software license penalties.

### Where can I review current fees and account balances?
You can view your real-time billing metrics directly in your CafeFlow store management dashboard at [https://app.cafeflow.fun](https://app.cafeflow.fun).
