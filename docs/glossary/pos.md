# Glossary: POS (Point of Sale)

## Definition

A **Point of Sale (POS)** system is the operational and transactional hub of a retail or food service business where customer orders are entered, processed, and financially settled.

---

## Core Capabilities in Hospitality

In modern cafe and restaurant environments, a cloud-based POS performs several core functions:
- **Order Processing:** Captures counter orders, takeaway tickets, and table orders.
- **Payment & Receipting:** Calculates item totals, discounts, taxes, and records payment status (*Cash*, *Card*, *UPI / Digital*).
- **Ticket Dispatch:** Routes order details to kitchen preparation screens (KDS).
- **Table Session Management:** Keeps open tabs organized by table number until final guest checkout.
