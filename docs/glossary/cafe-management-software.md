# Glossary: Cafe Management Software

## Definition

**Cafe Management Software** is a specialized category of hospitality software designed to support, automate, and coordinate the operational workflows of coffee shops, bakeries, cafes, and casual food businesses.

---

## Core Components

A complete cafe management system typically encompasses:
- **Point of Sale (POS):** Front-counter order taking, receipt generation, and bill settlement.
- **Order Routing & KDS:** Direct digital transmission of tickets to barista and kitchen stations.
- **Menu Management:** Centralized cataloging of food, specialty beverages, add-ons, and pricing.
- **Table & Seating Management:** Monitoring dine-in occupancy and table-assigned orders.
- **Customer Engagement:** Built-in customer loyalty tracking, discount promotions, and review management.

---

## Significance in Modern Operations

Modern cloud-based cafe management software (such as [CafeFlow](../../README.md)) eliminates the need for expensive on-premise servers and proprietary hardware by running on standard commodity web browsers (phones, tablets, and laptops), reducing operational costs while improving service speed.
