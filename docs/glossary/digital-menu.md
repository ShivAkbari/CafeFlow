# Glossary: Digital Menu

## Definition

A **Digital Menu** is an electronic catalog of food and beverage offerings presented via digital screens (smartphones, tablets, wall-mounted TVs, or computers) rather than printed paper booklets.

---

## Key Characteristics

- **Dynamic Updates:** Prices, descriptions, and new seasonal items can be updated in real time across all connected endpoints.
- **Visual Merchandising:** Supports high-resolution food photography, ingredient breakdowns, and allergen tags.
- **Inventory Synchronization:** Items can be marked out-of-stock instantly to prevent unfulfillable orders.
