# Glossary: Customer Loyalty Program

## Definition

A **Customer Loyalty Program** in the food and beverage industry is a structured customer retention mechanism that rewards patrons for frequent visits, repeat purchases, or spending milestones.

---

## Modern Digital Implementation

Traditional loyalty programs relied on paper punch cards or separate plastic key tags, which customers frequently misplaced. Modern digital loyalty solutions (such as the system built into [CafeFlow](../../README.md)):
- Track customer visits and rewards using the customer's mobile phone number.
- Seamlessly integrate rewards at POS checkout or within mobile QR ordering carts.
- Feature gamified rewards, such as digital scratch cards and milestone discount vouchers.
