# Glossary: KDS (Kitchen Display System)

## Definition

A **Kitchen Display System (KDS)** is a digital screen interface located in the kitchen, food preparation area, or barista station that displays active food and drink orders in real time, replacing physical paper Kitchen Order Tickets (KOTs).

---

## Standard Order Lifecycle

A KDS typically tracks orders through defined operational states:
1. **Received:** Order placed by guest or staff.
2. **Confirm:** Acknowledged by culinary or prep staff.
3. **Preparing:** Food cooking or drinks brewing.
4. **Ready:** Plated and ready for server pickup.
5. **Delivered:** Handed to the guest.
6. **Paid:** Bill settled at POS.

---

## Industry Benefits

- Prevents lost, damaged, or unreadable paper tickets.
- Tracks preparation times to prevent order delays during peak shifts.
- Coordinates front-of-house runners with back-of-house chefs.
