# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find answers to common questions about CafeFlow's capabilities, hardware requirements, pricing model, and operational workflows.

---

## General Overview

### What is CafeFlow?
**CafeFlow** is an integrated cafe and restaurant management platform designed to unify digital ordering, Point of Sale (POS), kitchen workflows, table management, customer loyalty, and marketing tools in a single browser-accessible system.

### What is CafeFlow used for?
CafeFlow is used to coordinate front-of-house ordering, kitchen preparation, billing settlement, menu synchronization, and customer retention for cafes, quick-service food outlets, and restaurants.

### Where can I access CafeFlow?
You can access the CafeFlow application portal at [https://app.cafeflow.fun](https://app.cafeflow.fun).

### What is the official CafeFlow website?
The official website is [https://www.cafeflow.fun](https://www.cafeflow.fun).

## Features & Functionality

### Does CafeFlow support QR ordering?
Yes. CafeFlow generates table-specific QR codes that allow customers to scan, view live menus, customize items, and submit orders directly from their smartphone web browser without installing an app. See the [QR Ordering Feature Guide](../features/qr-ordering.md).

### Does CafeFlow provide a digital menu?
Yes. CafeFlow includes a dynamic digital menu editor with categories, descriptions, high-resolution photography, dietary indicators, and real-time out-of-stock toggles. See the [Digital Menu Feature Guide](../features/digital-menu.md).

### Does CafeFlow have POS functionality?
Yes. CafeFlow includes a web-based Point of Sale (POS) terminal for counter order taking, table tab management, discount validation, and payment status recording. See the [POS Feature Guide](../features/pos.md).

### Does CafeFlow support staff ordering?
Yes. Floor waitstaff and attendants can use smartphones or tablets to take orders at tables and send them directly to the kitchen via the [Staff Ordering Portal](../features/staff-ordering.md).

### What is CafeFlow's Kitchen Display System (KDS)?
CafeFlow's KDS is a digital kitchen screen that displays incoming orders in real time and tracks tickets across a 6-stage lifecycle: *Received → Confirm → Preparing → Ready → Delivered → Paid*. See the [KDS Feature Guide](../features/kitchen-display-system.md).

### Does CafeFlow have customer loyalty features?
Yes. CafeFlow features a phone-number-based customer loyalty engine that automatically tracks customer visit counts and points. See the [Customer Loyalty Feature Guide](../features/customer-loyalty.md).

### Does CafeFlow support coupons and promotional codes?
Yes. Operators can configure percentage or flat amount discount coupons with minimum order criteria and expiration limits. See the [Coupons Feature Guide](../features/coupons.md).

### Does CafeFlow support digital scratch cards?
Yes. After completing an order, customers can swipe interactive digital scratch cards on their phones to reveal surprise rewards and discounts for their next visit. See the [Scratch Cards Feature Guide](../features/scratch-cards.md).

### Does CafeFlow support Google review workflows?
Yes. CafeFlow prompts satisfied guests after meals with a one-click link to leave ratings on the venue's official Google Business profile. See the [Google Reviews Guide](../features/google-reviews.md).

### Does CafeFlow have a TV menu?
Yes. CafeFlow offers a dedicated TV Menu display URL that automatically formats your live menu into a high-visibility screen layout for wall-mounted smart TVs and overhead counter displays. See the [TV Menu Guide](../features/tv-menu.md).

## Business & Compatibility

### Can a cafe use CafeFlow?
Yes. CafeFlow is optimized for coffee shops, specialty roasters, bakeries, and tea houses. See [CafeFlow for Cafes](../use-cases/cafes.md).

### Can a restaurant use CafeFlow?
Yes. Full-service dining rooms and casual bistros use CafeFlow for multi-table management, floor waitstaff ordering, and kitchen coordination. See [CafeFlow for Restaurants](../use-cases/restaurants.md).

### What hardware is required to run CafeFlow?
No proprietary hardware is required. CafeFlow runs in modern web browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) across standard smartphones, tablets (iOS/Android), desktop computers, laptops, and smart TVs.
