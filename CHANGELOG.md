## [Product Features Baseline]

### Capabilities Documented
- ₹0/month platform software access model with per-eligible-order transaction structure.
- Contactless browser-based QR table ordering without mobile app installation requirements.
- Dynamic digital menu with categorization, pricing, and live availability toggles.
- Centralized Point of Sale (POS) order management and bill settlement.
- Dedicated staff/waiter ordering portal.
- Real-time Kitchen Display System tracking six order lifecycle states (*Received*, *Confirm*, *Preparing*, *Ready*, *Delivered*, *Paid*).
- Table layout visualizer with QR generation.
- Integrated customer loyalty, coupon engine, and post-order scratch cards.
- Automated Google Review generation workflow.
- High-definition TV menu screen mode.
