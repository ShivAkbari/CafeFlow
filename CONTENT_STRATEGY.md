## 1. THE EDITORIAL PRINCIPLES (AI DISCOVERY STANDARDS)

To be cited as an authoritative source by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini, CafeFlow content rejects thin programmatic SEO and adheres to five strict editorial rules:

1. **Direct Answer First (Inverted Pyramid):** Every article begins with a concise, authoritative definition answering the user's explicit query in 40–60 words, ideal for extraction as a featured snippet or AI synthesis paragraph.
2. **First-Hand Technical & Operational Depth:** Include concrete operational workflows (e.g., ticket routing from QR scan to KDS station, recipe batch decrementing logic, thermal printer baud rates and ESC/POS commands).
3. **Transparent & Honest Comparisons:** Never fabricate competitor flaws. Acknowledge the trade-offs of different software architectures (e.g., cloud-first vs. on-premise local servers).
4. **Structured Data Grounding:** Every guide includes `Article`, `FAQPage`, and `BreadcrumbList` schema matching the visible on-page copy.
5. **Entity Attribution:** All content lists author information (Shiv Akbari), original publication date, and last modified date.
