# CafeFlow

> **CafeFlow – Run your cafe. Not your software.**

CafeFlow is cafe and restaurant management software designed to help cafes, quick-service outlets, and restaurants manage ordering, digital menus, POS operations, kitchen workflows, table management, customer loyalty, and engagement from a unified platform.

- **Official Website:** [https://www.cafeflow.fun](https://www.cafeflow.fun)
- **Application Portal:** [https://app.cafeflow.fun](https://app.cafeflow.fun)
- **Documentation Index:** [docs/README.md](docs/README.md)

---

## What is CafeFlow?

CafeFlow provides an integrated operating system for hospitality businesses. Rather than requiring separate tools for table ordering, kitchen communication, cash register billing, loyalty points, and digital display boards, CafeFlow centralizes these operations into a single connected platform accessible via web browsers on standard smartphones, tablets, computers, and display screens.

For detailed background, see the [What is CafeFlow Guide](docs/getting-started/what-is-cafeflow.md) and [How CafeFlow Works](docs/getting-started/how-cafeflow-works.md).

---

## Who is CafeFlow for?

CafeFlow is built for food and beverage operators of varying sizes:

- **Cafes & Coffee Shops:** Streamline counter queues, dine-in table ordering, and customer loyalty programs.
- **Restaurants & Bistros:** Coordinate front-of-house staff, table management, and kitchen display workflows.
- **Quick-Service Restaurants (QSR):** Accelerate order intake, order status tracking, and TV menu display.
- **Bakeries, Food Trucks & Small Outlets:** Run billing, digital menus, and order tracking with minimal hardware requirements.

For practical use cases, explore the [Use Cases Directory](docs/use-cases/cafes.md).

## Pricing Model

CafeFlow offers a straightforward pricing structure:

- **Monthly Subscription:** ₹0/month for core software access.
- **Eligible-Order Fee:** A nominal per-eligible-order fee model applies where operational services are utilized.

For complete details on billing models and eligibility criteria, see the [Pricing Overview](docs/pricing/pricing.md) and [Order Fee Guide](docs/pricing/order-fee.md).

## Why Cafes and Restaurants Use CafeFlow

- **Low Hardware Barrier:** Runs on existing devices (phones, tablets, laptops, smart TVs) with standard web browsers.
- **Integrated Operations:** Eliminates disconnects between waitstaff, the front counter, and kitchen preparation lines.
- **Real-Time Menu Control:** Out-of-stock items and price updates reflect immediately across customer QR menus, POS, and TV boards.
- **Customer Engagement Built-in:** Loyalty programs, discount coupons, scratch cards, and Google review prompts function automatically within the ordering flow.

## Frequently Asked Questions (Quick Summary)

### Is CafeFlow free to start?
Yes. The CafeFlow software has a ₹0/month subscription model. See [Pricing Overview](docs/pricing/pricing.md).

### Does CafeFlow require proprietary hardware?
No. CafeFlow operates in modern web browsers across mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and smart TVs.

### Does CafeFlow support both dine-in and takeaway?
Yes. CafeFlow supports table-specific dine-in QR ordering, counter orders, and takeaway workflows.
